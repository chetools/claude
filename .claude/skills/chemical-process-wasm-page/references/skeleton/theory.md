# Theory source skeleton

The builder converts this file into the breakdown panel (Section 5 of
the skill). One headed section per subunit, in diagram order; the
builder inserts the section's sub-process diagram after its heading.
Mathematics uses $...$ inline and $$...$$ display spans; every symbol
is defined at or before its first use, with SI units. Currency amounts
in prose are written so that no $...$ span forms around them, or the
span contains no TeX command, underscore, caret, or equals sign.

## Feed preparation

The symbol $\dot{m}_{fresh}$ denotes the fresh feed mass flow rate
(in kg/h). The feed section raises the feed to reaction pressure and
removes the catalyst poisons named in the research dossier [1].

## Synthesis loop behaviour

The recycle ratio $R$ (dimensionless) is defined as
$R = \dot{m}_{recycle} / \dot{m}_{fresh}$, where $\dot{m}_{recycle}$
denotes the recycle gas mass flow rate (in kg/h).
