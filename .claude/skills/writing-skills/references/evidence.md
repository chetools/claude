# Evidence and limits

This file is not loaded for writing. It holds the evidence, corpus sizes, and limits for the core and the reference files. Rule numbers refer to version 7 of the package; the house rules added in version 7 (core rules 5–7, 33, 35, `figures.md`, `ste100.md`, and heads C6–C7 in `ai-check.md`) come from the author's standing style guide, not from a coded corpus.

## Method

Each rule was coded from a measured corpus: documents were read against a fixed rubric, constructions were counted, and a rule states a construction that the coded documents use at the stated scale. Advice literature was not a source for the main rules. Two codebook anchors for the business corpus (Barbara Minto, *The Pyramid Principle*; Gene Zelazny, *Say It with Charts*) name forms the corpus documents use; the rules state the measured forms, not the books' advice.

Provenance tags ("[Test amendment]", "[Textbook amendment]", frequency brackets) stood in the loaded files of earlier versions and were moved out of the loaded package at the tiered revision, so the loaded files carry rules only. Case codes in the tags: T1 is the tutorial case of the combined test, T2 the abstract case, T3 the recommendation memo case, T4 the executive summary case, T5 the slide storyline case; D1 is the equation diagnostic case and D2 the letter diagnostic case; S6 is an equation case from an earlier paired test of a shortened draft, with the same verdict as D1 on symbol placement; C1–C6 are the cases of the tiered-package confirmation retest.

## Corpora

STEM papers: 37 papers from *Nature* and *Science*, top citation rank, 2015–2024; an 8-paper mid-cited comparison set from the same journals. Flow measures (sentence openings, connectives, sentence lengths) were computed on 34 of the 37 papers.

Instructional writing: 22 items from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and DeepMind (tutorials, explainers, and documentation).

Distinctive patterns: 26 further *Nature* and *Science* papers and 16 further instructional and research-blog items (Anthropic, DeepMind, OpenAI, Google Research, Microsoft Research, Meta AI, Distill, and independent research blogs), coded for low-frequency constructions that carry a result, a scope condition, and an interpretation with no loss in one move. Core rules 25–31 and 34 rest in part on this pass.

Equations: an oversample of 10 equation-bearing papers, 19 coded equations, 103 symbol groups. The equations reference rests on this pass and on the main corpus.

Business and consulting: 22 documents. Six long-form articles from *Harvard Business Review* and *MIT Sloan Management Review*; seven documents from McKinsey & Company, the McKinsey Global Institute, Boston Consulting Group, and Bain & Company; five chief executive officer letters (Berkshire Hathaway, JPMorgan Chase, Amazon); a 4-document comparison set from the mid-readership business press.

Textbooks: a contrast study of five publicly available engineering textbooks, with detailed coding of three canonical chemical-engineering textbooks (Seborg, Edgar, Mellichamp and Doyle, *Process Dynamics and Control*; Smith, Van Ness, Abbott and Swihart, *Introduction to Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics*, 8th edition; Seader, Henley and Roper, *Separation Process Principles*): stratified samples of 160 coded passages and 30 rewrite comparisons, plus full-text counts. Core rule 32 and `textbook.md` rest on this study; a textbook guideline stands only where the coded books agree.

## Paired tests

Specimen pairs were written at equal word counts, one version with a skill and one version without a skill, on identical briefs, and coded blind with labels randomised. Recognition strings in the rules (for example in core rules 10, 11, and 32) record forms that occurred in the no-skill versions at the stated counts while the with-skill versions were favoured on the same feature.

A later test compared the combined single file against the two predecessor skills used separately, on five decision cases (a STEM tutorial, a STEM abstract, a recommendation memo, an executive summary, a slide storyline) and two diagnostic cases (an equation passage, a chief executive officer letter). The combined version was judged more effective in three of the five decision cases; its losses produced the amendments recorded in the provenance tags. The diagnostic loss on the letter case is the measured cost of holding the letter patterns in the appendix: one case, decided on features that the main business rules partly cover.

A paired test and a confirmation retest then compared the tiered package (core plus condition-loaded references) against the single-file form; the tiered package was installed after that test. A trigger test in the same study found that inference markers in closing frame sentences accounted for all frame-side errors in eight test documents; core rule 14 states the resulting rule.

A long-form pair (a route-comparison report at 1,200 words per version, written from one frozen fact base, one version under the skill and one version with no skill) is published with the paired samples page linked from `README.md`. The pair is a demonstration specimen, not a blind test: the versions were not coded blind, and no verdict beyond the stated feature counts is recorded for it.

## Limits

- The pilots measure text: coded features and coder verdicts on those features. No pilot measures reader outcomes, comprehension, or decision quality.
- Each condition in the paired tests ran once per case. A three-to-two split across five cases does not exclude ordinary variation.
- The combined-skill test used one coder and reports no second-coder reliability statistic. The business pilot reports no second-coder reliability statistic.
- Frequencies are frequencies over the coded sets named here. A construction frequent in these corpora is not thereby frequent in a field corpus, and a rule states a form to use, not a rate to match.
- Corpus documents are published works under their publishers' copyright and are not included in this package. Rules, counts at corpus level, and the constructed specimens on the paired samples page are the package's content.
- Version 7 (compressed core, merged checklist) was checked against version 6 on three briefs (a Couette-flow tutorial, a methanol synthesis-loop report section, and a plant-scale recommendation memo): one Opus draft per version and brief, scored blind against the version 6 rules by one Opus checker per brief. Major content findings were 0 for version 7 and 2 for version 6; version 7 ranked first on 2 of the 3 briefs, and minor findings were 34 against 31. One draft per cell does not exclude ordinary variation.
- These are working rules. A rule is revised when a further coded corpus or a paired test counts against it.
