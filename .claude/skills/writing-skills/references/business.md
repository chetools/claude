# Business documents [load only for a business document]

Activation: a request for business or consulting writing, or an assigned business document type (a recommendation memo or report, an executive summary, a slide storyline). Content type alone does not activate this file. In a mixed document, technical sections follow the core and non-technical sections follow this file.

Overrides, business documents only: core rules 16 and 17 do not govern the opening (B1 and B2 do); the core's no-persuasion clause in rule 1 is replaced by the screened persuasion of B9; core rule 34 governs journal-form exhibits and B6 consulting-form exhibits; core rule 20 governs claim strength, and a document that cannot restate its claim closes by a move under B5.

B1. The bottom line stands before the evidence: a summary block, a deck sentence, or an opening paragraph states the finding or the recommendation first.
B2. The opening also performs a frame, without labelling it: situation, complication, and the question the document answers, in that order, or a named alternative frame (a failure base rate, a valuation puzzle, a challenge catalogue). B1 and B2 are both performed in the opening paragraph or paragraphs; situation material placed after the recommendation does not perform the frame.
B3. The evidence base (survey, sample, or use-case base, with its count) is named where the finding is stated, in the summary or the exhibit notes, not only in a methods section.
B4. A recommendation is an action with a named owner class. Timing and quantified benefit stand in the evidence sections or the closing, not in the recommendation sentence.
B5. Where a claim is not restated, the closing is next steps, questions for the reader's team, a checklist, or an invitation. A formal decision ask with a named decision maker and date is used only on request.
B6. Consulting-form exhibit titles state the finding with its number or comparison, and a source line names the data base and respondent count. In a slide storyline, the opening title states the recommendation complete (action, scope, held items, decision point), and each support line belongs to the title it follows.
B7. A trade-off is stated in one sentence with both numbers: benefit and cost, or gain and risk.
B8. A period result is one sentence that states its components, or a headline figure followed in the next sentence by its signed components at the same scale.
B9. Persuasion screen. Measured recommendation and framing devices are used; devices that misstate evidence are not: a closing claim stronger than the opening claim with no evidence added between them; a superlative with no comparison set; a certainty opener with no evidence pointer in the same sentence; an analogy in closing position with no stated boundary.

Completion check: bottom line present (B1); frame performed in the opening (B2); owner class named for each recommendation (B4); evidence base named where the finding is stated (B3); in a slide storyline, each support line checked against its own title.
