# Figures [load for a document with figures, plots, or tables]

Text rules for figures. Drawing rules (sizes, line weights, colour, export) are in the separate visual style specification, where one is installed.

F1. One message per figure; two messages get two figures or two panels.
F2. No title inside the figure; the caption (core rule 34) carries the message, and a chart title that repeats the caption is removed.
F3. A figure with its caption is understandable without the main text. After the takeaway, the caption gives, in order: what is shown, symbol definitions, conditions, the sample count n (the number of observations), and the error-bar definition, where these apply.
F4. Axis labels read "Quantity, symbol (unit)", for example "Pressure drop, Δp (kPa)". For an interactive figure with its caption next to the plot, "symbol (unit)" is used, with the symbols defined in the caption or the adjacent prose. Axis titles, tick labels, and legend entries are short.
F5. Every abbreviation, legend entry, and axis label used on a page appears at least once on that page next to its full form, so a reader who searches for the short form finds it defined.
F6. Symbols in a figure match the text exactly: variables italic, units and descriptive subscripts upright, vectors bold.
F7. Legends sit outside the plot area; direct labels are preferred where they fit.
F8. A log scale is used only by decision, with decades labelled as powers (10^2).
F9. A table is used for a comparison across shared attributes; its title or caption follows core rule 34, and its column heads carry units.
