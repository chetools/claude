# Appendix: single-corpus patterns

Patterns in this appendix are not main rules. Each rests on one corpus and is stated with its conditions. A document may use a pattern where its conditions hold; a document is not incomplete for omitting one.

## Chief executive officer letter patterns (corpus: 5 letters)

Evidence base: the five chief executive officer letters in the business corpus (Berkshire Hathaway, JPMorgan Chase, Amazon). Neither pattern occurs in full form in the long-form or consulting corpora, and neither is a general business rule.

L1 (predecessor business skill, rule 21). Error admission with the cost named. The admission stands in the document that reports the result, names the failure and its owners, and states the cost in the admission passage. Evidence: both Berkshire Hathaway letters, in full form; a partial form in the JPMorgan Chase 2023 letter, where a reported gain is qualified by its itemised masked costs.

L2 (predecessor business skill, rule 22). Results before narrative, in a fixed house form. The letter opens with the period results as a bundled sentence or a decomposition, before interpretation, and refills the same template each period; closing moves are gratitude or invitation, with no restated claim. Evidence: results-first openings in 4 of 5 letters (the Berkshire Hathaway 2023 letter opens with a tribute); the template repeated across both JPMorgan Chase letters and, as a closing formula, in the Amazon letter.
