title: Fenske–Underwood–Gilliland shortcut for a multicomponent column
parts: a,b,c,d,e,f
---
A saturated-liquid feed of 100 kmol/h is to be distilled. Its composition (mole fractions) and the relative volatilities (constant, relative to n-pentane) are:

| Component | z_F | α |
|---|---|---|
| propane | 0.05 | 4.2 |
| n-butane (light key) | 0.35 | 2.0 |
| n-pentane (heavy key) | 0.40 | 1.0 |
| n-hexane | 0.20 | 0.45 |

98 % of the n-butane is to be recovered in the distillate, and 97 % of the n-pentane is to be recovered in the bottoms. Assume all the propane goes to the distillate and all the n-hexane to the bottoms. The column has a total condenser.

(a) Calculate the distillate flow rate D, in kmol/h.
(b) Calculate the minimum number of theoretical stages N_min (Fenske equation, based on the key components).
(c) Calculate the root θ of the Underwood feed equation that lies between the relative volatilities of the two keys.
(d) Calculate the minimum reflux ratio R_min.
(e) For R = 1.3 R_min, calculate the number of theoretical stages N using the Gilliland correlation in the Eduljee form, Y = 0.75(1 − X^0.5668), with X = (R − R_min)/(R + 1) and Y = (N − N_min)/(N + 1). Report the non-integer value (N includes the partial reboiler).
(f) Calculate the ratio N_R/N_S of rectifying to stripping stages from the Kirkbride equation, log₁₀(N_R/N_S) = 0.206 log₁₀[(z_HK/z_LK)(x_B,LK/x_D,HK)²(B/D)].
