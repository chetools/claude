title: Laminar oil flow in a tube
parts: a,b,c
---
An oil (density ρ = 880 kg/m³, viscosity μ = 0.12 Pa·s) flows at a volumetric rate of 1.5 L/s through a horizontal, smooth, straight tube of inside diameter 40 mm and length 25 m. The flow is fully developed; neglect entrance effects.

(a) Calculate the Reynolds number.
(b) Calculate the Darcy friction factor.
(c) Calculate the frictional pressure drop over the 25 m length, in kPa.
