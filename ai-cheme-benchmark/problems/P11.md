difficulty: Extremely hard
title: McCabe–Thiele design with a partially vaporized feed
parts: a,b,c,d,e
---
A binary mixture with constant relative volatility α = 2.4 is separated in a distillation column with a total condenser and a partial reboiler, assuming constant molar overflow. The feed is 100 kmol/h with light-component mole fraction z_F = 0.45, and it is partially vaporized with q = 0.6 (q = fraction of the feed that is liquid). The products are x_D = 0.96 and x_B = 0.04. The reflux ratio is R = 1.5 R_min.

(a) Calculate the distillate flow rate D, in kmol/h.
(b) Calculate the minimum reflux ratio R_min.
(c) Calculate the operating reflux ratio R.
(d) Step off equilibrium stages from the top of the column (starting at x_D), switching from the rectifying to the stripping operating line at the optimal feed location. Calculate the total number of theoretical stages, counting the partial reboiler as a stage. Report it as a non-integer: whole stages plus the fraction of the last stage, where the fraction is (x_{N−1} − x_B)/(x_{N−1} − x_N).
(e) State the optimal feed stage, counted from the top (the stage whose liquid composition first falls below the x-coordinate of the intersection of the two operating lines).
