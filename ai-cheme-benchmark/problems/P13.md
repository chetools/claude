title: Pumped two-loop water network with a demand node
parts: a,b,c,d,e
---
A centrifugal pump takes water from a large open suction tank S and delivers it through a two-loop network to a large open elevated tank T. Water is at 20 °C: ρ = 998.2 kg/m³, μ = 1.002×10⁻³ Pa·s. Take g = 9.81 m/s². The tank water levels stay constant.

Layout (all pipes are circular and flow is steady):

- The suction pipe runs from tank S to the pump inlet. The pump outlet is node A. The pump and node A are at elevation 0 m. The free surface of S is at +1.0 m.
- Pipe 1 joins A to B, pipe 2 joins A to C, pipe 3 joins B to C, pipe 4 joins B to D, pipe 5 joins C to D (so the loops are A–B–C and B–C–D).
- Pipe 6 joins D to tank T and enters T below its free surface. The free surface of T is at +14.0 m.
- A user withdraws a steady 16.0 L/s from node B (to atmosphere; the withdrawal pipe is not part of the network above). There is no other withdrawal.

Node elevations: B = 8.0 m, C = 4.0 m, D = 10.0 m.

| Pipe | Length (m) | Inside diameter (mm) | Roughness ε (mm) | Sum of minor-loss coefficients ΣK |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suction | 30 | 200 | 0.045 | 1.4 |
| 1 (A–B) | 300 | 125 | 0.26 | 4.0 |
| 2 (A–C) | 450 | 100 | 0.045 | 8.0 |
| 3 (B–C) | 200 | 75 | 0.15 | 3.0 |
| 4 (B–D) | 350 | 100 | 0.26 | 2.0 |
| 5 (C–D) | 400 | 125 | 0.045 | 5.0 |
| 6 (D–T) | 150 | 150 | 0.045 | 1.0 |

Each ΣK already includes every fitting, the entrance and the exit of that pipe, and is based on the mean velocity in that pipe: a pipe's total head loss is (f L/D + ΣK) V²/(2g). Use the Darcy–Weisbach equation with the Colebrook equation for f in each pipe separately, using the actual Reynolds number of each pipe. Neglect velocity heads at the nodes and in the tanks, so the pressure at a node is p = ρ g (H − z) with H the piezometric head and z the node elevation. The head added by the pump (rise of piezometric head from pump inlet to pump outlet) follows the pump curve H_p = 80 − 12000 Q_p², with H_p in m and the pump flow Q_p in m³/s. The pump efficiency is 70 %.

(a) Calculate the pump flow Q_p, in L/s.
(b) Calculate the head added by the pump, in m.
(c) Calculate the magnitude of the flow in the internal pipe 3 (B–C), in L/s, and state its direction, either "from B to C" or "from C to B".
(d) Calculate the gauge pressure at node C, in kPa.
(e) Calculate the pump shaft power, in kW.
