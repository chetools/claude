title: Underdamped second-order step response
parts: a,b,c,d,e
---
A process has the transfer function G(s) = 4/(9s² + 2.4s + 1), time in minutes, deviation variables. Initially at steady state, it receives a step change of magnitude 2 in its input at t = 0.

(a) Calculate the damping factor ζ.
(b) Calculate the overshoot, as a percentage of the final change.
(c) Calculate the time of the first peak, in minutes.
(d) Calculate the maximum value reached by the output deviation y′.
(e) Calculate the decay ratio (the second peak overshoot divided by the first peak overshoot).
