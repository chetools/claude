title: Isothermal compressible flow in a gas pipeline
parts: a,b,c,d
---
Methane (molar mass 16.04 kg/kmol, viscosity μ = 1.1×10⁻⁵ Pa·s, ideal gas) flows isothermally at 288 K through a horizontal steel pipeline with inside diameter 0.30 m, length 40 km and absolute roughness ε = 0.046 mm. The inlet pressure is 5.0 MPa (absolute) and the outlet pressure is 3.0 MPa (absolute). Use R = 8314 J/(kmol·K). Treat the flow as isothermal, compressible, steady and fully developed; include the acceleration (kinetic-energy) term in the isothermal pipe-flow equation, and use the Colebrook equation for the Darcy friction factor (note that the Reynolds number is constant along the pipe).

(a) Calculate the mass flow rate, in kg/s.
(b) Calculate the Reynolds number.
(c) Calculate the Darcy friction factor.
(d) Calculate the gas velocity at the outlet, in m/s.
