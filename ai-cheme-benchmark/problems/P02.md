title: Pump power for a tank-to-tank transfer
parts: a,b,c,d
---
Water (ρ = 998 kg/m³, μ = 1.00×10⁻³ Pa·s) is pumped at 0.025 m³/s from an open tank to a second open tank whose free surface is 12 m higher. The line is 150 m of commercial steel pipe with inside diameter 0.10 m and absolute roughness ε = 0.046 mm. The sum of all minor-loss coefficients (entrance, fittings, valves and exit), based on the pipe velocity, is ΣK = 5.5. Use the Colebrook equation for the friction factor. Both free surfaces are at atmospheric pressure and their velocities are negligible.

(a) Calculate the Reynolds number in the pipe.
(b) Calculate the Darcy friction factor.
(c) Calculate the total head the pump must deliver, in m.
(d) Calculate the pump shaft power, in kW, for a pump efficiency of 72 %. Use g = 9.81 m/s².
