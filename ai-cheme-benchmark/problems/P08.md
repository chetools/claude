title: Stability limit of a PI loop on a third-order process
parts: a,b,c
---
A process G_p(s) = 1/((s + 1)(2s + 1)(5s + 1)), time in minutes, is controlled by a PI controller G_c(s) = K_c(1 + 1/(τ_I s)) with τ_I = 4 min. The valve and measurement transfer functions are 1, and K_c > 0.

(a) Using the Routh array of the closed-loop characteristic equation, calculate the maximum controller gain K_c,max for which the closed loop is stable.
(b) Calculate the frequency of the sustained oscillation, in rad/min, when K_c = K_c,max.
(c) If the PI controller is replaced by a proportional-only controller with K_c = 3, calculate the steady-state offset (setpoint minus final output) after a unit step change in setpoint.
