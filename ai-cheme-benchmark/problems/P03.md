title: Three-reservoir problem
parts: a,b,c,d
---
Three large reservoirs A, B and C have free-surface elevations of 120 m, 100 m and 70 m respectively. Each is connected by its own pipe to a common junction J:

- Pipe 1 (A–J): length 1500 m, inside diameter 0.30 m
- Pipe 2 (B–J): length 800 m, inside diameter 0.20 m
- Pipe 3 (C–J): length 1200 m, inside diameter 0.25 m

All pipes are cast iron with absolute roughness ε = 0.26 mm. The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity ν = 1.0×10⁻⁶ m²/s. Use the Darcy–Weisbach equation with the Colebrook friction factor in every pipe, take g = 9.81 m/s², and neglect minor losses and velocity heads (so the head at J equals its piezometric head).

(a) Calculate the piezometric head at the junction J, in m.
(b) Calculate the flow rate in pipe 1, in L/s.
(c) Calculate the magnitude of the flow rate in pipe 2, in L/s, and state whether water flows into or out of reservoir B.
(d) Calculate the flow rate in pipe 3, in L/s.
