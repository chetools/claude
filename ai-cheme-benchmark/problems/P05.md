title: First-order step response
parts: a,b,c
---
A process is described by the transfer function G(s) = Y′(s)/U′(s) = 2.5/(8s + 1), with time in minutes and deviation variables. Initially at steady state, the input is given a step increase of 4 units at t = 0.

(a) Calculate the final (new steady-state) change in the output.
(b) Calculate the output deviation y′ at t = 10 min.
(c) Calculate the time, in minutes, at which the output reaches 95 % of its final change.
