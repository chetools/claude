title: Countercurrent absorber by the Kremser equation
parts: a,b,c
---
A gas stream of 100 kmol/h containing 2.0 mol % acetone in air is fed to the bottom of a countercurrent tray absorber. Pure water enters at the top. 95 % of the acetone must be absorbed. The system is dilute: take the gas and liquid molar flows as constant through the column and the equilibrium as y = 1.8x (mole fractions). The water flow is 1.5 times the minimum water flow.

(a) Calculate the minimum water flow rate, in kmol/h.
(b) Calculate the absorption factor A = L/(mG) at the operating water flow.
(c) Calculate the number of theoretical stages required (Kremser equation; report the non-integer value).
