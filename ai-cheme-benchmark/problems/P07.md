title: Ultimate gain, Ziegler–Nichols tuning and stability margins of a dead-time process
parts: a,b,c,d,e,f
---
A feedback loop consists of a controller and a process G_p(s) = 1.8 e^(−2.5s)/(12s + 1), time in minutes. The valve and measurement transfer functions are both 1.

(a) Calculate the ultimate (crossover) frequency ω_u, in rad/min, for proportional-only control, i.e. the frequency at which the phase angle of G_p is −180°.
(b) Calculate the ultimate gain K_u.
(c) Calculate the ultimate period P_u, in minutes.
(d) Calculate the Ziegler–Nichols PI settings: K_c = K_u/2.2 and τ_I = P_u/1.2 (in minutes). Report K_c and τ_I.
(e) For the loop with this PI controller, G_c(s) = K_c(1 + 1/(τ_I s)), calculate the gain margin (as a ratio, not in dB).
(f) For the same loop, calculate the phase margin, in degrees.
