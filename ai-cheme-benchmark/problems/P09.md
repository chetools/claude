title: Minimum stages and minimum reflux for a binary column
parts: a,b
---
A binary mixture with a constant relative volatility α = 2.5 is fed to a distillation column as saturated liquid with light-component mole fraction z_F = 0.40. The distillate is to contain x_D = 0.95 and the bottoms x_B = 0.05 (light-component mole fractions). The column has a total condenser.

(a) Calculate the minimum number of theoretical stages at total reflux (use the Fenske equation, not stage stepping; report the non-integer value, counting a partial reboiler as a stage).
(b) Calculate the minimum reflux ratio R_min = (L/D)_min.
