topic: Process Dynamics and Control
difficulty: Frontier
title: Ziegler–Nichols PID, stability margins and peak sensitivity of a third-order dead-time loop with a sensor lag
parts: a,b,c,d,e,f
---
A feedback loop has the process G_p(s) = 2.0 e^(−1.5s)/((6s + 1)(2s + 1)(s + 1)), time in minutes. The valve transfer function is G_v(s) = 1 and the measurement (sensor) transfer function is G_m(s) = 1/(0.5s + 1). The controller G_c(s) acts on the error (setpoint minus measurement), and the open-loop transfer function is L(s) = G_c(s) G_v(s) G_p(s) G_m(s). All frequencies are in rad/min. Use the exact dead-time term e^(−θs) throughout, with no Padé or other rational approximation. Phase angles are continuous (not wrapped to ±180°) and start at 0° at ω = 0 for the process-plus-sensor part.

(a) With a proportional-only controller G_c = K_c, calculate the ultimate frequency ω_u (rad/min), the lowest frequency at which the phase of G_v G_p G_m is −180°.
(b) Calculate the ultimate gain K_u and the ultimate period P_u = 2π/ω_u (minutes).
(c) Calculate the Ziegler–Nichols PID settings K_c = 0.6 K_u, τ_I = P_u/2 and τ_D = P_u/8 (minutes), for the ideal (non-interacting, parallel-gain) form G_c(s) = K_c(1 + 1/(τ_I s) + τ_D s), with no derivative filter. Report K_c, τ_I and τ_D.
(d) For the loop with this PID controller, calculate the gain margin (as a ratio, not in dB). It is evaluated at the lowest frequency ω_pc where the phase of L(iω) first reaches −180°, starting from −90° at ω → 0⁺ (the integral action); also report ω_pc.
(e) Calculate the phase margin, in degrees, at the gain-crossover frequency ω_gc where |L(iω)| = 1 (it is unique); also report ω_gc.
(f) Calculate the maximum sensitivity M_s = max over all ω > 0 of |1/(1 + L(iω))|, and the frequency ω at which it occurs.
